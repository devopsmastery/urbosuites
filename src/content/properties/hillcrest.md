Hillcrest is the suite that workationers keep extending. The 100Mbps fibre connection, the ergonomic desk chair by the window, and the view of Pashan Sus Road below make it easy to stay productive — then step onto the balcony when you need a reset.

Open-plan studio with a Super king sized bed, navy sofas, 55-inch Smart TV, and a fully stocked bar kitchen.

What makes Hillcrest distinct is its slightly different light — east-facing, so the sun comes in warm and the evenings are particularly good for chai or peacock spotting from balcony.

The rain shower bathroom has marble finishes. The building gym is on the podium level, and the cafeteria serves breakfast and light meals through the day.

**Good to know:** Hillcrest has hosted back-to-back business travellers for months at a time — it's equipped for extended stays with a proper luggage rack, blackout blinds, and a quiet corridor location.
