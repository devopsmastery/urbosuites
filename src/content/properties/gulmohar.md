Step off the elevator and the hill is right there — a solid wall of green that fills the balcony from edge to edge. No concrete, no neighbours' balconies, just sky and trees and the occasional cloud rolling through at eye level.

The studio is open-plan: a plush sofa-cum-bed faces a 40-inch Smart TV, and a King Size bed with fresh linens sits beneath a stunning floral wall mural. The kitchen has everything you need for a quick meal — induction stove, microwave, kettle, and a fridge stocked with chai ingredients on arrival.

The marble bathroom has a full rain shower. The building gym, cafeteria, and covered parking are shared amenities available to all guests.

**Practical details:** 24/7 security, a basement laundromat, and meeting rooms on the 1st floor. VJ IndiLife Building C is a 30-minute drive from Pune railway station and 45 minutes from Pune Airport. Pashan market and Sus Road restaurants are a 5-minute walk.
