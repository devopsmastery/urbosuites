Pashan sits at a quiet edge of Pune that most visitors overlook in favour of Koregaon Park or Kalyani Nagar. That's precisely why it works so well for workationers.

## Why Pashan?

The neighbourhood sits between the Baner tech corridor and the Sus Road café strip, which means you're close enough to Hinjewadi for day trips to client offices but far enough away that the traffic noise doesn't follow you home. The Pashan lake walk takes 25 minutes at a leisurely pace and is the kind of morning ritual that makes extended stays feel intentional rather than just economical.

## Where to Work

**Cafés with reliable Wi-Fi:**
- *The Daily Grind, Sus Road* — opens at 7am, power sockets at every seat, filter coffee that's worth the walk.
- *Driftwood Café, Baner* — 10-minute drive, excellent espresso, a back room that stays quiet until noon.
- *Pagdandi Books Chai Café, Baner* — if you need a change of scene, the book-lined walls are good for thinking.

**Coworking spaces nearby:**
- *Innov8, Baner* — day passes available, private phone booths, the usual amenities.
- *91Springboard, Baner* — larger community, good for networking if that's part of the trip.

For days when you'd rather not commute at all, UrboSuites comes with 100Mbps fibre, an ergonomic desk setup, and a balcony with a hill view — which turns out to be a surprisingly effective replacement for a standing meeting.

## Eating Well Without Planning Too Hard

Pashan market (a 5-minute walk from VJ IndiLife) has a good subzi vendor, a reliable bakery, and a chai stall that opens before 6am. The Suites kitchenette handles everything else: induction stove, microwave, fridge, and enough utensils for a proper meal.

For evenings when you want someone else to cook: Sus Road has expanded significantly in the past two years. Noteworthy stops include Burma Burma for Southeast Asian, Prem's for South Indian breakfast served until 11am (they're strict about it), and The Flour Works for reliable wood-fired pizza.

## Getting Around

An auto to Baner takes 10 minutes and costs around ₹60. Hinjewadi Phase 1 is 20 minutes by bike or 30 by auto. The Pune-Mumbai expressway ramp is 15 minutes away if you need a weekend escape.

## What to Do on Downtime

- **Pashan Lake:** Morning walks, migratory birds in winter, and a chai stall at the entry point that's been there since before anyone can remember.
- **IISER Pune campus:** Beautiful tree-lined paths, open to visitors, 10-minute walk.
- **Mulshi Dam:** A 40-minute drive that rewards you with reservoir views and a decent sunset. Worth the petrol.
